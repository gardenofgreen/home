Simple Plant Care Guide

A quick reference for anyone bringing home a plant from my garden — or just starting their own journey.

🌸
Queen of the Night
Epiphyllum hookeri
Easy to grow
☀️ SunlightBright indirect
💧 WaterEvery 1–2 weeks
🌡️ ClimateWarm, humid
🪴 SetupHanging pot ideal
🌸 BloomsOnce a year, at night
💡 Blooms only open after dark and last just one night — incredibly rare and beautiful. Keep in a spot with good airflow.
🪴
Succulents
Various genera
Easy to grow
☀️ SunlightBright indirect–full
💧 WaterEvery 1–2 weeks
🌡️ ClimateWarm, tolerant
🪴 SetupPot preferred
📏 GrowthSlow and steady
💡 Always use pots with drainage holes. Overwatering is the #1 killer — when in doubt, wait.
👻
Ghost Plant
Graptopetalum paraguayense
Very easy
☀️ SunlightFull sun to partial
💧 WaterEvery 2–3 weeks
🌡️ ClimateWarm, drought-tolerant
🪴 SetupPot or ground
🎨 ColorTurns pink in full sun
💡 Leaves propagate easily — just lay a leaf on dry soil and it will sprout roots on its own within weeks.

My Story

Why I Love Plants

Plants have a way of teaching patience, presence, and care — three things I've learned from every seed I've ever planted.

Plants don't care about your schedule. They grow on their own timeline, and that's the most grounding lesson I've ever learned.
01
Patience is Everything

A dragon fruit cutting won't fruit for a year or more. Gardening taught me to stop rushing outcomes and trust the process. Nature has its own pace — and it's always worth waiting for.

02
No Shortcuts, Ever

I've never used pesticides or synthetic fertilizers. What you grow organically just feels different — and tastes different. The slow, natural way is the only way in my garden.

03
Share the Green

The best part of gardening is sharing it. When a cutting I grew ends up rooting in someone else's yard across the country, that feels like magic. Plants connect people.
